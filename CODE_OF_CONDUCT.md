# Code of Conduct

Bellbird aims to maintain an open, respectful, and constructive project community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and consider differences in background, experience, and perspective.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, and give and receive it constructively.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy, and credit other people's work.
- Take responsibility for the impact of your actions and help repair harm when it occurs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Personal attacks, insults, slurs, or unwelcome sexualized language or attention.
- Sharing private or confidential information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project spaces or retaliating against someone who raises a concern.

## Scope

This code applies to Bellbird project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, comments, documentation, and any channel officially connected with the project. It also applies when someone represents Bellbird in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this code, contact the maintainer through the [project contact page](https://diegopn.github.io/#contato) to request a private channel. Do not post incident details in a public issue, pull request, or discussion, or include sensitive details in the initial contact.

Reports will be reviewed as soon as practical and handled discreetly. Information will be shared only with people who need it to assess and respond to the report.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and applying these standards. Depending on the situation, actions may include a private warning, removing or declining content, temporarily limiting participation, or permanently excluding someone from project spaces. Responses will be proportionate to the conduct; steps may be skipped when the behavior is serious. Decisions will be explained when appropriate.
